# Call of Cthulhu Seventh Edition source manifest

## Authority

- `coc7e.investigators-handbook` — _Call of Cthulhu Seventh Edition Investigator's Handbook_. Required, official player source.

The handbook supplies investigator creation, occupations, Skills, equipment, organizations, player-facing play procedures, and optional experienced-investigator packages. Definition records retain the source ID and printed page. Display text is original, concise guidance rather than reproduced source text.

## Deliberate boundaries

- The 7E Keeper workspace is unavailable until an authoritative Seventh Edition Keeper Rulebook is supplied.
- Mythos creatures, a player-browsable spell/tome catalog, automatic development-phase resolution, Keeper procedures, and scenario material are not inferred from the Investigator's Handbook.
- Mythos discoveries, spells, and tomes may be retained as custom or future Keeper-granted records only.
- No PDF page, illustration, map, logo, font, or example investigator is packaged with Arcane Record.

## Reviewed ambiguities

- Starting Luck uses `3d6 × 5`, following the handbook's procedure and quick reference.
- Optional creation methods, starting Skill caps, transferable specialization benefits, human-potential bonuses, and experienced packages are opt-in character settings. They never alter an existing investigator merely by being visible.
- Narrative consequences of pushed rolls, insanity, and Reality Checks are recorded but never selected by the application.
