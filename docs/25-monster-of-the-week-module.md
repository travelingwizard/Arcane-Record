# Monster of the Week module

Monster of the Week is implemented as two browser-local records: a Hunter and
an authoritative Keeper campaign. Hunters may link to a campaign and retain an
export snapshot, but Arcane Record never uploads or synchronizes campaign data.

The revised core book supplies the twelve core playbooks and rules. Tome of
Mysteries is optional and disabled by default; it adds the Gumshoe, Hex,
Pararomantic, Searcher, alternative Weird moves, and phenomenon Mysteries.

The guided creator records playbook, rating package, identity, choices, gear,
and histories. The sheet tracks Luck, harm, experience, improvements, current
Mystery state, and 2d6 move history. The roller performs arithmetic and result
band classification only. Fictional outcomes and Keeper moves remain table
decisions.

Keeper records organize a campaign, roster, multiple Mysteries, six-stage
countdowns, threats, arcs, custom moves, session questions, and awards. Revision
metadata and deletion warnings protect browser-local links.

Source text is represented by short original summaries and citations. Source
PDFs, artwork, example mysteries, and fan playbooks are not runtime assets.
