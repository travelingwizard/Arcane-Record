# Legend in the Mist source manifest

## Rules authority

- *Legend in the Mist Core Book*, 491-page supplied PDF.
- Hero creation and theme references: pp. 68–137.
- Action and status procedures: pp. 138–181.
- Development and fulfillment: pp. 182–191.
- Optional Ways of Magic: pp. 194–225.
- General Store and backpack references: pp. 128–133.

## Character-facing inventory

The runtime catalog contains 20 themebooks, 30 official tropes, 120 core theme
kits, eight optional Ways of Magic, and 32 magic theme kits. The earlier
planning count of 20 tropes was corrected during source audit: the core book
presents 30, three per page across pp. 78–87.

Definitions use the `litm.*` namespace, original concise summaries, and
book/page provenance. Narrator-only Challenges, adventures, creatures, Oracle
material, artwork, logos, fonts, and long source passages are excluded.

## Ambiguity and automation boundary

- Broadness, fictional relevance, campaign fit, and custom Might remain
  Narrator rulings.
- The roll helper performs arithmetic and outcome-band lookup only. It never
  chooses Effects or Consequences.
- Optional Ways of Magic are inert until enabled on an individual Hero.
- Unverified details remain reference-only and cannot drive automatic changes.
