# Miseries & Misfortunes source manifest

Books 1-3 are required. Books 4-6 are optional and disabled for new records. Arcane Record stores concise original summaries and citations, not source pages, artwork, maps, fonts, adventures, or extended prose.

| ID | Book | Use | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mm.book1` | Miseries & Misfortunes | Core system, mentalities, fortune, reputation, Mortal Coil, social and martial combat | Required |
| `mm.book2` | Les Fruits Malheureux | Creation, lifepaths, advancement, virtues/flaws, equipment | Required |
| `mm.book3` | The Sacred & the Profane | Gnosis, grimoires, magic, theology, lifepaths | Required |
| `mm.book4` | More Miseries | Moments, plots, favor, duello, horses, disease, equipment | Off |
| `mm.book5` | Catalunya | Character-facing Catalan lifepaths, mentalities, and magic | Off |
| `mm.book6` | Paris 1648 | Character-facing Paris mentalities, lifepaths, factions, and patronage | Off |

Life expectancy remains private campaign data. Automated helpers perform objective arithmetic only; experience-condition interpretation, magical outcomes, and historical consequences remain human rulings.
