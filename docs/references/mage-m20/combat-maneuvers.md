# Core Combat Maneuvers Reference

Use this file as the local reference for standard combat maneuvers in Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary Edition.

Descriptions are concise app summaries. Keep the source citations visible in the interface.

## General Maneuvers

| Name | Description | Source | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aborting an Action | Change a declared action to a defensive action, usually by spending Willpower or succeeding on a Willpower roll. | Mage 20 | 416 |
| Ambush | Attack from concealment or surprise. The target must notice the attacker in time to defend normally. | Mage 20 | 416 |
| Blind Fighting / Blind Fire | Attempt close or ranged attacks without normal vision, with increased difficulty. | Mage 20 | 416–417 |
| Flank or Rear Attack | Attack from a position the defender cannot easily cover, reducing or preventing normal defense. | Mage 20 | 417 |
| Movement | Walk, run, or sprint during combat. Faster movement may modify attacks and defenses. | Mage 20 | 417 |
| Multiple Actions | Divide a dice pool or use another permitted method to perform more than one action in a turn. | Mage 20 | 417 |
| Targeting | Aim for a specific body part or object. Smaller targets increase difficulty but may provide extra effect or damage. | Mage 20 | 417–418 |

## Defensive Maneuvers

| Name | Description | Source | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Block | Use Dexterity + Brawl or Martial Arts to stop a close-combat attack with the body. | Mage 20 | 418 |
| Dodge | Use Dexterity + Athletics to evade an attack by moving away, dropping, or reaching cover. | Mage 20 | 418 |
| Parry | Use Dexterity + Melee or Martial Arts and a weapon or shield to deflect a close-combat attack. | Mage 20 | 418 |
| Desperate Defense | Devote the turn to repeated defensive actions with reduced multiple-action penalties. | Mage 20 | 418–419 |

## Close-Combat Maneuvers

| Name | Description | Source | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clinch | Grapple and damage an opponent with Strength + Brawl. Either character may later damage or attempt escape. | Mage 20 | 419 |
| Close Distance | Move inside the reach of a longer weapon. Weapon-length differences can penalize the shorter-armed fighter until distance is closed. | Mage 20 | 419 |
| Disarm | Strike or seize an opponent's weapon to knock it from their hand without directly damaging them. | Mage 20 | 419 |
| Hold | Restrain an opponent without inflicting damage. The victim may attempt to escape on a later action. | Mage 20 | 419 |
| Jab Pistol | Press or jab a pistol into a nearby target before firing, increasing damage but risking disarm. | Mage 20 | 419–420 |
| Kick | A harder unarmed attack that generally inflicts Strength +1 bashing damage. | Mage 20 | 420 |
| Multiple Opponents | Fighting several attackers increases the difficulty of attacks and defenses. | Mage 20 | 420 |
| Strike / Punch | A normal unarmed attack using Dexterity + Brawl and Strength-based bashing damage. | Mage 20 | 420 |
| Sweep | Use a leg or weapon to damage and knock an opponent down. | Mage 20 | 420 |
| Tackle | Rush and slam into a target, causing damage and risking knockdown for both combatants. | Mage 20 | 420–421 |
| Weapon Strike | A normal close-combat weapon attack using Dexterity + Melee and the weapon's damage. | Mage 20 | 421 |

## Ranged Combat Maneuvers

| Name | Description | Source | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aiming | Spend one or more turns lining up a shot to gain additional attack dice, subject to weapon and target limits. | Mage 20 | 421 |
| Automatic Fire | Empty a large portion of a weapon's ammunition to gain many attack dice at increased difficulty. | Mage 20 | 421 |
| Cover | Use terrain or objects to become harder to hit, sometimes at the cost of making your own attacks harder. | Mage 20 | 421–422 |
| Multiple Shots | Fire more than once by using the multiple-action rules, limited by the weapon's rate. | Mage 20 | 422 |
| Range | Point-blank shots are easier; attacks beyond normal range are harder; attacks beyond maximum range fail. | Mage 20 | 422 |
| Reloading | Replace or refill ammunition. Reloading normally requires an action. | Mage 20 | 422 |

| Short Burst | Fire a short burst to gain attack dice at increased difficulty without emptying the entire weapon. | Mage 20 | 422–423 |

## App Notes

- These are reference maneuvers, not limited character purchases.
- Players may mark maneuvers as favorites for quick access.
- Allow custom combat maneuvers.
- Keep Do maneuvers separate because they are learned character options.
- Do not automate attack or damage rolls in this feature.
