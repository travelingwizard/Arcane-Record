# Mage supplement review

## Delivery status

The source-availability foundation is implemented. The exhaustive catalog audit
is not complete. `source-manifest.json` lists every current definition and its
review status; regenerate with `node scripts/generate-mage-source-manifest.mjs`.
Do not treat known point costs as verified mechanics.

Verified in this pass: Book of the Fallen pp. 117-119 (six previously uncosted
Traits), Lore of the Traditions pp. 33-34 (Qi Manipulation and Qi tools), and
p. 45 (Palimpsest). NPC-only Abyssal Lunatic remains reference-only.

## Unresolved rules and content

- Sphere Inept, Book of Secrets p. 94: prose and printed cost table must be
  reconciled by an explicit table ruling. Do not silently apply a formula.
- Enlightened Grimoire is community content. Its index pages are not sufficient
  citations for verified Rote mechanics. Appendix rules require independent opt-in.
- Existing Lore of the Traditions citations above the supplied book's page range
  and citations to indexes need entry-by-entry correction.
- Complete character-facing catalog extraction from all six books remains open.
- Child creation, Resonance/Synergy, faction extensions, Wonder crafting,
  Grimoire point rules, and Digital Web workflows remain subsequent phases.

## Persistence

Mage schema 12 adds source preferences. Older characters retain their records;
core is enabled and supplements are disabled for browsing until selected.
Sources never delete or deactivate previously selected Traits. Browser defaults
apply only to newly created characters, not migrations.

No PDFs, illustrations, portraits, or long source passages are redistributed.
