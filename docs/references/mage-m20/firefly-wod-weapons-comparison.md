# Firefly Dies weapons comparison

## Source and compatibility

Compared on 2026-08-07 against the
[Firefly Dies World of Darkness Weapons reference](https://www.fireflydies.com/WoDSRD/Weapons.html).
The page contains 168 weapon-table rows sourced primarily from *World of
Darkness: Armory*.

Those traits belong to the newer World of Darkness rules line and are not
directly compatible with Mage 20's classic World of Darkness statistics. The
138 added records therefore preserve compact factual traits as an unverified
reference preview and explicitly say that Storyteller conversion is required.
They do not calculate damage, change character ratings, or enforce rules.

The comparison does not reproduce the site's descriptive notes. It retains
item names, compact statistics, the cited book/page, and a compatibility note.

## Comparison result

- 168 rows reviewed;
- 138 missing entries added;
- 9 rows already represented by the same named catalog item;
- 16 rows matched an existing equivalent under a broader or Mage 20 name;
- 5 shotgun-gauge modifier rows were not standalone equipment and were not
  imported.

Same-name rows already represented: Great Sword, Katana, Mace, Sap, Nunchaku,
Hatchet (melee and thrown), Brass Knuckles, and Shuriken.
