The following is a comprehensive list of Merits and Flaws for **Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary Edition**, organized by category and including descriptions, point values, and source citations.

### **Physical Merits**

* **Acute Senses (1 or 3 pt.):** Reduces the difficulty of all Perception-based rolls involving a single sense (1 pt.) or all five senses (3 pt.) by -2. [Mage 20, p. 642; BoS, p. 35]
* **Alcohol /Drug Tolerance (1 to 2 pt.):** Allows a Stamina roll (difficulty 7) to shake off intoxication. The 1 pt. version applies to natural substances; the 2 pt. version covers lab-synthesized ones. [BoS, p. 35]
* **Ambidextrous (1 pt.):** No penalty for using two weapons or performing tasks with the off-hand. [BoS, p. 36]
* **Cast-Iron Stomach (1 pt.):** Character can eat nearly anything organic without ill effects. [BoS, p. 36]
* **Catlike Balance (1 pt.):** Reduces the difficulty of physical feats involving balance (like tightrope walking) by -2. [BoS, p. 36]
* **Hyperflexible (1 pt.):** Reduces the difficulty of rolls involving flexibility (like escaping bonds) by -2. [BoS, p. 36]
* **Light Sleeper (1 pt.):** Character only needs four hours of sleep and wakes up instantly alert. [BoS, p. 36]
* **Noble Blood (1 pt.):** Distinctive features mark the character as part of a powerful lineage, commanding respect or attracting enemies. [BoS, p. 36]
* **Enchanting Feature (2 pt.):** A magnificent physical feature (eyes, voice, etc.) that provides a social edge in appropriate situations. [BoS, p. 36]
* **Physically Impressive (2 pt.):** Adds two dice to social rolls involving intimidation due to a massive or menacing build. [BoS, p. 37]
* **Poison Resistance (2 pt.):** Adds two dice to Stamina rolls to resist natural or synthesized toxins. [BoS, p. 37]
* **Poker Face (2 pt.):** A deadpan expression that reveals nothing; reduces difficulty of intimidation and subterfuge rolls by -2. [BoS, p. 38]
* **Daredevil (3 pt.):** Adds three dice to non-combat/non-magickal rolls involving high-risk physical stunts. [BoS, p. 38]
* **Hypersensitivity (3 pt.):** Character is extraordinarily open to physical sensations (cannot be taken with Insensate to Pain). [BoS, p. 38]
* **Nightsight (3 pt.):** Character ignores penalties for low-light conditions on visual perception rolls. [BoS, p. 38]
* **Huge Size (4 pt.):** Character is over seven feet tall and 300 pounds; adds one Health Level and -1 difficulty to Strength rolls. [BoS, p. 38]
* **Insensate to Pain (5 pt.):** Character ignores dice-pool penalties from wounds until death, though physical impairment remains. [BoS, p. 38]
* **Too Tough to Die (5 pt.):** Character can soak lethal damage (but not aggravated damage). [Mage 20, p. 644; BoS, p. 39]
