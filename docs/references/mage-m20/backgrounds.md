# Mage M20 Backgrounds

The following list of Backgrounds for **Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary Edition** is compiled from the core rulebook and *The Operative's Dossier*. These Backgrounds represent resources, connections, and internal qualities available to Awakened and Enlightened characters.

## Standard Backgrounds

- **Allies (1 to 5+ pt.):** Represents friends, helpful animals, or minor spirits who can aid you in times of need. Each dot typically provides one moderate ally or two minor sidekicks. [Mage 20, p. 303]
- **Alternate Identity (1 to 5 pt.):** An established alias or false identity that allows you to operate under a different name. [Mage 20, p. 304]
- **Arcane / Cloaking (1 to 5 pt.):** A mysterious ability to remain unrecognized or forgotten; it imposes penalties on the dice pools of those trying to find or investigate you. [Mage 20, p. 305]
- **Avatar / Genius (1 to 5 pt.):** A measure of the strength and presence of your Awakened or Enlightened internal guide. It determines how much Quintessence your character can store and channel. [Mage 20, p. 305]
- **Backup (1 to 5+ pt.):** Access to unAwakened personnel from a larger organization (like a gang or the Technocracy) whom you can call upon for assistance. [Mage 20, p. 306]
- **Blessing (1 to 5 pt.):** An unusual non-combat talent or twist of fate linked to a figure or force that favors you. [Mage 20, p. 307]
- **Certification (1 to 5 pt.):** Official licenses, permits, or professional degrees that grant legal authority or access to specialized equipment. [Mage 20, p. 308]
- **Chantry / Construct (1 to 5 pt.):** Membership in or ownership of a mystic or Technocratic stronghold. Characters can pool points to build more elaborate facilities with integrated effects. [Mage 20, p. 308; OpD, p. 91]
- **Contacts (1 to 5+ pt.):** Social networks and individual information sources who provide news or minor favors. [Mage 20, p. 309]
- **Cult (1 to 5 pt.):** A group of dedicated believers who trust in you and can gather to lend their faith to your large-scale rituals. [Mage 20, p. 310]
- **Demesne (1 to 5 pt.):** A personal inner dream-space where your character is at home. [Mage 20, p. 310]
- **Destiny (1 to 5 pt.):** A sense of great purpose that allows you to regain Willpower points once per session during a crisis. [Mage 20, p. 311]
- **Dream / Hypercram (1 to 5 pt.):** The ability to tap into collective memories or archives to access Abilities your character does not normally possess. [Mage 20, p. 312]
- **Enhancement (1 to 5 pt.):** Cybernetic or biotech modifications that boost Physical Attributes, Perception, or Intelligence. This Background costs **two points per dot** and often incurs permanent Paradox or genetic flaws. [Mage 20, p. 312]
- **Fame (1 to 5 pt.):** Notoriety in the Sleeper world that provides social clout but makes it difficult to remain inconspicuous. [Mage 20, p. 313]
- **Familiar / Companion (1 to 5 pt.):** A non-human entity (spirit, animal, or construct) that shares a mystical bond with the mage and offers special assistance. [Mage 20, p. 314]
- **Influence (1 to 5+ pt.):** Social clout and the ability to pull strings within mortal institutions. [Mage 20, p. 316]
- **Legend (1 to 5 pt.):** A connection to a mythic archetype or pop-culture icon that allows you to refresh Quintessence once per story. [Mage 20, p. 317]
- **Library (1 to 5+ pt.):** Access to specialized information, archives, or esoteric texts. [Mage 20, p. 318]
- **Mentor (1 to 5 pt.):** An Awakened or Enlightened elder who provides guidance, training, and occasional aid. [Mage 20, p. 318]
- **Node (1 to 5+ pt.):** Possession of a place of power that generates ambient Quintessence and Tass. [Mage 20, p. 319]
- **Past Lives (1 to 5 pt.):** Memories from previous incarnations that can provide extra dice for various tasks. [Mage 20, p. 320]
- **Patron (1 to 5 pt.):** A powerful and influential benefactor who provides resources and political protection. [Mage 20, p. 320]
- **Rank (1 to 5 pt.):** A title of importance within a mortal institution, such as the military, clergy, or a corporation. [Mage 20, p. 321]
- **Requisitions (1 to 5+ pt.):** **(Technocracy only)** The ability to temporarily "buy" advanced gear and hardware from the Union's divisions. [Mage 20, p. 321]
- **Resources (1 to 5+ pt.):** Financial assets, including cash flow, credit, and significant property. [Mage 20, p. 322]
- **Retainers (1 to 5 pt.):** Skilled followers, servants, or employees who answer directly to you. [Mage 20, p. 323]
- **Sanctum / Laboratory (1 to 5 pt.):** A private, dedicated space optimized for your magickal or scientific work. This Background costs **two points per dot**. [Mage 20, p. 323]
- **Secret Weapons (1 to 5 pt.):** **(Technocracy only)** Access to experimental hypertech inventions provided to operatives willing to be "guinea pigs". [Mage 20, p. 325]
- **Spies (1 to 5+ pt.):** Information networks that deal in sensitive or secret data. [Mage 20, p. 325]
- **Status (1 to 5 pt.):** Your reputation and standing among your Awakened or Enlightened peers. [Mage 20, p. 325]
- **Totem (1 to 5 pt.):** A powerful spirit ally for shamanic characters that provides wisdom and Ability boosts. This Background costs **two points per dot**. [Mage 20, p. 326]
- **Wonder (1 to 5 pt.):** Possession of a Talisman, Fetish, or Device that contains its own inherent magickal power. [Mage 20, p. 328]
