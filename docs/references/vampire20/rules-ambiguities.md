# Vampire 20 rules ambiguities

- Chronicle-specific Path access, rare Disciplines, and antagonist bloodlines
  require a recorded Storyteller approval rather than automatic eligibility.
- The revised ghoul Discipline progression is treated as optional and requires
  Storyteller approval, a suitable blood donor, and a teacher.
- Background increases remain story-controlled unless an individual purchase
  receives explicit Storyteller approval.
- Catalog entries whose mechanics have not been independently reviewed remain
  citation-only and do not drive automatic pricing.
