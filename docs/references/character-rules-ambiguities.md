# Curseborne and City of Mist rules review

This file is the blocking-rule register for the completion project. A known
cost and a checked page citation do not, by themselves, authorize automation.
`docs/references/character-source-inventory.json` contains source headings that
still need entry-level review.

## Curseborne

- Core p. 203 supplies four Injury Levels with 2/2/2/1 boxes; the summary on
  p. 231 describes three levels. The sheet follows p. 203 and keeps armor and
  Taken Out separate.
- Core p. 241 higher-start packages are treated as alternatives, not cumulative
  awards. Entanglement 3-4 needs a recorded Storyguide ruling for the unclear
  Edge-dot award before it can be automated.
- Core p. 179's difficulty-zero sidebar is treated as the specific exception to
  the general zero-hit result. Ordinary Enhancement still needs a rolled hit.
- The Lethean Blade on Accursed Adventures p. 46 refers to Pain Absorption on
  p. 47 and uses unclear influence wording. It remains citation-only and cannot
  apply effects automatically.
- Core headings, Families, Role Paths, Edges, spells, and equipment remain in
  inventory status until costs, prerequisites, exact player eligibility, and
  interactions are reviewed at entry level.

## City of Mist

- Player's Guide and Shadows & Showdowns themebook citations are indexed, but
  individual question sets and improvement lists are not yet represented. A
  themebook may be selected manually; the app must not imply full automation.
- The newer supplied player-move sheet is the move reference. The earlier 2017
  sheet is not blended into it. Supplement exceptions and Dynamite outcomes
  remain unavailable until reconciled.
- Fictional breadth, relevance, MC choices, Tier 5/6 consequences, narrative
  transitions, and Moment benefits require explicit human decisions.
- Gatekeeper theme requirements and development exceptions need entry-level
  review before catalog-dependent automation is enabled.

## Release rule

Resolve an item by adding a concise ruling, citation, catalog representation,
and tests. If the printed sources remain genuinely unclear, store the table's
decision as a specific approval rather than choosing silently.
