# Wraith 20 rules ambiguities

- Legacy supplement mechanics remain unavailable to automated costs until they
  are reconciled against Wraith 20. Their descriptive records may be retained.
- Storytellers decide Guild membership, Initiate Arcanos access, Haunt effects,
  Fetter relevance, Passion fulfillment, and narrative consequences.
- Psyche and Shadow experience remain separate histories. Historical prices
  are never recalculated after a catalog correction.
- Medium and Spectre creation budgets must be driven only by verified published
  frameworks; missing procedures are not inferred from antagonist stat blocks.
