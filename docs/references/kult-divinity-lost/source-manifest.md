# KULT: Divinity Lost source manifest

## Runtime sources

| Source ID | Authority | Use | Runtime asset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `kult.core` | Core Rules, Original Kickstarter Edition | Rules, Enlightened archetypes, advancement references | No |
| `kult.players-guide` | Player's Guide | Player archetypes and concise suggestion prompts | No |

The General, Sleeper, Aware, and Enlightened PDF sheets were used only to verify
field hierarchy and progression groupings. No PDF page, logo, illustration,
font, icon, border, or authored example character is packaged with Arcane
Record.

Suggestion catalog entries are short adapted summaries with stable IDs and
page citations. They populate individual editable records and do not certify a
complete character. Sensitive suggestions remain hidden until enabled for the
individual character.

- Automatic archetype legality or option-count enforcement.
- A gameplay dice roller or GM campaign workspace.
