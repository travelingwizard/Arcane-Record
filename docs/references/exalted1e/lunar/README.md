# Exalted 1e — Lunar source intake

**Status: primary sourcebook fully inventoried; three cross-splat passes
complete (sorcery/artifice, the Storyteller's Companion, and Savage Seas);
further cross-splat supplements outstanding.**

inventoried cover-to-cover for character-creation-relevant material, a
further pass has added the *Savant and Sorcerer* / *Book of Three Circles*

"Artifacts of the Waves" naval-artifact catalog. See
[docs/18-exalted1e-splat-release.md](../../18-exalted1e-splat-release.md)

Chapter Four: Traits (pp. 94-115 / PDF 97-118) is now also **fully
inventoried** (24 new rows):

- **Expanded Backgrounds.** Five altered Backgrounds carried over from core
  Exalted with Lunar-specific flavor text only (no new dot table): Artifact
  (moonsilver's shapeshifting properties and the Silver Pact's possessiveness
  over it), Backing (rare for Lunars; Pact rank itself is tracked by Renown,
  not Backing), Followers (drawn from barbarian tribes or beastman societies
  who see Lunars as living gods), Manse (young Lunars must seize a Manse from
  a god/demon/outcaste or quest to earn an elder's), and Mentor (a re-flavored
  0-5 dot table measuring closeness to the character's Silver Pact elder
  rather than the mentor's personal power) — pp. 96-98 (PDF 99-101).
- **New Backgrounds.** Three Lunar-exclusive Backgrounds with their own dot
  tables: Cult (Essence and temporary Willpower income from worshipers; the
  legible dot table only runs 0-4 — no 5-dot line was found and this is
  flagged for a second look before promotion), Heart's Blood (the number of
  animal/human forms a Lunar knows at the start of play, with separate 0-5
  dot scales for animals and for Heart's Blood (Human)), and Renown (bonus
  starting points toward the separate Renown Trait; Casteless may not take
  it) — pp. 98-100 (PDF 101-103) — plus the supporting Regions sidebar
  (p. 101/PDF 104) that guides which animals a Heart's Blood purchase may
  represent by the character's homeland.
- **Castes.** This edition of *Exalted: The Lunars* has only **three**
  player Castes — Full Moon (pp. 102-103/PDF 105-106), Changing Moon
  (pp. 104-105/PDF 107-108), and No Moon (pp. 106-107/PDF 109-110) — plus the
  uninitiated Casteless state (p. 108/PDF 111), *not* the five-Caste
  structure (adding Waxing/Waning Moon) introduced in later editions; the
  chapter's own "Caste" framing text (p. 109/PDF 112) explicitly says a
  Lunar's essence is fixed into "one of the three castes." Each Caste row
  covers Trials, Totem Animals, Anima Banner, Anima Effects, Caste
  Attributes, Associations, Sobriquets, and Concepts. The anima-effect grant
  every Lunar gets regardless of Caste (five minor 1-mote Tell/Caste-Mark/
  anima tricks) was inventoried separately as Lunar Universal Anima Effects,
  and the Peripheral-Essence-spend visibility table was inventoried as Lunar
  Anima Banners — both p. 109-110 (PDF 112-113).
- **The Great Curse.** This splat's Limit-Trait analog (p. 109/PDF 112),
  triggered by acting against a Lunar's primary Virtue or failing a
  Willpower roll against full moonlight, plus its four Virtue-linked
  inner-beast thralls — Curse of the Whipped Dog (Compassion), Curse of the
  Heartless Weasel (Conviction), Curse of the Drunken Monkey (Temperance),
  and Curse of the Raging Bull (Valor) — pp. 109-110 (PDF 112-113), each
  inventoried as its own row.
- **Face and Renown.** The Lunar social-rank system (pp. 111-115/PDF
  114-118), inventoried as three rows: the Face Trait itself (six named
  ranks, Urrach-ya "the Nonperson" through Shahan-ya "the Greatest," plus the
  Face Requirements table of minimum Renown and other prerequisites per
  rank), the four-category Renown Trait (Succor/Compassion,
  Mettle/Conviction, Cunning/Temperance, Glory/Valor — distinct from the
  Renown *Background*, which merely grants bonus starting points toward it),
  and the tlak ceremony/losing-face/respecting-face social procedures.
- A short **Lunars and Regeneration** sidebar (p. 115/PDF 118) closes out
  the chapter, cross-referenced to the existing Deadly Beastman
  Transformation and Halting the Scarlet Flow Charm rows.

No dedicated Lunar-specific **Merits/Flaws** subsection was found anywhere in
Chapter Four; the chapter moves directly from New Backgrounds into
Castes/Great Curse/Face without one. This is confirmed absent, not merely
unreviewed — the chapter's opening statement says Merits/Flaws otherwise work
identically to core Exalted. Likewise, no dedicated **Tribe** write-up or
Tribe-as-Background section exists; Lunar tribes are addressed only through
the altered Backing/Followers text and general chapter flavor, not as a
discrete character-creation mechanic.

That Merits/Flaws gap is now filled from a second source book: the
**Exalted Players Guide**, Chapter One, "Merits and Flaws" (printed pp.
18-41, PDF pp. 21-44), has been transcribed in full — 88 rows (44 merits +
44 flaws) in `inventory/exalted-the-lunars.json` with the `pg-merit-*` /
`pg-flaw-*` id prefix. This chapter is **universal**, applying across all
five planned Exalted families (Lunar, Abyssal, Sidereal, Alchemical, Fair
Folk), so every row's `notes` field captures any splat-specific eligibility
or cost caveats verbatim rather than assuming universality. Highlights
relevant to Lunars: **Chimera** is a Lunars-only Flaw (a Lunar who has
swapped totem animals, gaining a hybrid Deadly Beastman appearance and
losing access to the Renown Background if truly Wyld-touched); **Known
Anathema**, **Permanent Caste Mark** (the Lunar tattoo is silver),
**Greater Curse** (reduces the Lunar's maximum Limit pool), and
**Throwback** are all Celestial-Exalted-only (Solar/Lunar/Sidereal) and
apply to Lunars; **Death-Taint** is Abyssals-and-Ghosts-only and does *not*
apply to Lunars; **Dark Fate** normally excludes only Sidereals (not
Lunars). The chapter closes with a full-page illustration (printed p.
42/PDF 43) and a short fiction piece (printed p. 43/PDF 44) before Chapter
Two, "The God-Blooded," begins on printed p. 44/PDF 45.

A few rows carry explicit `notes` flagging spots that need a closer look
before promotion (an illegible cost line on Cat-Falling Attitude, an
awkward cap-formula sentence on Hydra Head Attack that may be OCR/print
damage in the original, an ambiguous infection-resolution sentence on
Rabid Beast Bite, an awkward botch-consequence sentence on Grasping
Pseudopod Method, and Armor-Rending Claw Fist's mechanics text being cut
off mid-sentence at the bottom of its rendered page — the remainder needs
to be read off the next page before that row is considered fully
complete). Several Melee/Ranged Combat Charms also carry `notes` flagging
that their mechanics text spans a page break in the source (stat block on
one page, prose continuing on the next); these were transcribed as single
continuous Charms. The Defensive-III tree diagram (PDF p. 169/printed
p. 166) labels one node "Limb-Shedding Growth," but that Charm's own
header and running text (PDF p. 170/printed p. 167) both consistently
read "Limb-Shielding Growth" — the Charm's own text was trusted per the
standing rule to prefer a Charm's stated name over the tree-diagram
lettering. Pack-Saving Method's reduced-cost case references a Charm
named "Brotherhood of Lake and River" that has not yet been located
elsewhere in this book, so no `crossReferences` entry was added for it
yet. Bruise-Relief Technique's rate/cost sentence in the source reads
redundantly ("For every mote spent, the Exalt may remove one health
level of bashing damage per point of Stamina, at a cost of 1 mote of
Essence per health level") and is flagged in its `notes` for a second
look before promotion. Two Perception Charms (Sense-Borrowing Method
and Harmony With Reality Technique's tree ancestor) reference a
co-prerequisite Charm named "Pack-Forming Presence"; that Charm was
subsequently located in the Interaction and Knowledge III tree (printed
p. 190/PDF 193), and both Sense-Borrowing Method's and Pack-Saving
Method's `crossReferences` were updated accordingly, with their `notes`
removed. Ritual of Lunar Stability's own stat block states its Duration
in lunar months equal to permanent Essence, but its mechanics text
states the Wyld-reduction effect lasts a number of weeks equal to
permanent Essence — both units were transcribed faithfully as printed
and flagged in its `notes` for a closer look before promotion. Several
Interaction and Knowledge Charms also carry `notes` flagging illustration
cut-offs or garbled/redundant source phrasing (Chameleon Skin Disguise,
Lion Roar Method, Pack-Calling Cry) that were paraphrased as faithfully
as legible but are worth a second look before promotion.

### Savant and Sorcerer / Book of Three Circles (220 rows)

A later pass added the two cross-splat sorcery/artifice supplements that
this project's Abyssal and Sidereal inventories had already triaged, using
Lunar's own `terrestrial-circle-sorcery`/`celestial-circle-sorcery` Charms
(Chapter Five, Spirit and Sorcery-access Charms) as the enabling
prerequisites. Per this project's Solar Circle Sorcery eligibility rule
(Lunars do not have Solar Circle Sorcery access), no Circle of Adamant
content was inventoried. Most of this pass's rows were adapted from this
project's already-verified Exalted: The Sidereals transcription of the
identical sourcebook prose (same citation, mechanics, and cost; only each
row's `compatibility` text and cross-references were updated for Lunar), with
two wholly new Lunar-specific rows read directly from the source PDF where
neither the Abyssal nor Sidereal inventory had transcribed the equivalent
splat-specific section:

- **The complete Chapter Five Terrestrial and Celestial Circle spell
  catalog** (84 selectable spells, printed pp. 101-138/PDF 102-139,
  `spell-terrestrial-*`/`spell-celestial-*` ids), cross-referenced to
  `terrestrial-circle-sorcery`/`celestial-circle-sorcery`.
- A **102-entry Hearthstone catalog**, all selectable and cross-referenced
  to the Manse (altered for Lunars) Background: 15 Sample Hearthstones from
  Savant and Sorcerer Chapter Three (printed pp. 66-71/PDF 67-72) plus 87
  more from *Book of Three Circles* Chapter Six's 'long roll'
  (`hearthstone-botc-*` ids), reused from the Sidereal inventory's own
  page-by-page render of that image-based chapter.
- A **13-row Artifice and Enchantment catalog** from Savant and Sorcerer
  Chapter Two (printed pp. 23-41/PDF 24-42): the Artifact rating framework,
  the Drawback-subtype/alternate-power-source rules, the five-step creation
  process, two named Merits (Starting Workshop; **Moonsilver Initiate**,
  whose cost the book itself reduces to 2 points for Lunar Exalted), the
  Magical Materials catalog (moonsilver's forging ritual and its tie to the
  Silver Pact), Exotic Ingredients examples, and five selectable Sample
  Artifacts (Collar of Dawn's Cleansing Light, Mask, Ring of Being, Wings of
  the Raptor, Soul Mirror) — plus a wholly new **"In-Game Creation: the
  Lunar Exalted"** row (`artifact-creation-lunar-notes`) transcribed
  directly from the source PDF (printed pp. 35-36/PDF 36-37), covering how
  First Age Lunars crafted at remote forges under Luna's blessing and modern
  Lunars work in secret, isolated locations near the Wyld.
- A **13-row Warstriders appendix** (printed pp. 144-160/PDF 145-161): the
  overview/operation/benefits-limitations/maintenance framework rows, all
  five basic warstrider design classes as selectable Artifact-rated
  equipment (Scout, Common, Noble, **Royal** — explicitly attested to a
  "scant handful" of First Age Lunar pilots in the book's own text, one of
  the few classes with documented Lunar use — and Juggernaut), the
  weaponry-mounting/mundane-weapons/artifact-weapons reference catalogs, and
  the moonsilver-forged **Proteus Gauntlet** (which already grants Lunars
  its full Magical-Material bonus without the double-Essence surcharge
  non-Lunars pay).
- An **8-row skim of Chapter One (Magical Theory) and Chapter Four
  (Practical Summonings)**: the Limits of Sorcery, the Black and White
  Treatises acquisition mechanic, the Five Unavoidable Challenges initiation
  framework, and the demon/elemental summoning-procedure overview,
  social/political/licensing context, mass summoning project rules, and the
  Bound Spirit Limit Profile system — plus a wholly new **"Lunar Sorcery
  Initiation: the No Moon Monopoly"** row (`sorcery-lunar-initiation-notes`)
  transcribing Chapter One's "No Moons" sidebar (printed p. 15/PDF 16)
  directly from the source PDF: No Moons alone teach Celestial Circle magic
  (and only to other No Moons), every No Moon is expected to master it as
  quickly as possible, and any other Lunar caste must plead for Terrestrial
  Circle tutelage and keep secret any higher-circle knowledge it somehow
  acquires.
