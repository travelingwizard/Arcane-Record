# Call of Cthulhu Second Edition rules review

- Hit Points use the average of CON and SIZ. The app rounds a half point upward so the persisted track remains an integer; the extracted rule text does not state a general rounding direction at that passage.
- The 1920s Previous Experience procedure is exposed as an optional alternative workflow. Its numerous table outcomes require further catalog transcription before the app can apply them automatically; enabling the switch records the choice without inventing results.
- Occupation eligibility is recorded and reviewed, but unusual Keeper-created occupations require a Keeper Approved note rather than inferred skill lists.
- Mythos spells, tomes, entities, and insanity expressions remain Keeper-controlled. The app records objective values and grants but never chooses narrative outcomes.
- Ordinary experience improves a checked skill only when the improvement roll is above its rating. Cthulhu Mythos and derived characteristic rolls are excluded.
